Why it's built this way

One file, no install

The tools are single HTML files. Save it, double-click it, it runs in your browser. Works on a locked-down work laptop and in ten years when a vendor has shut down.

Your data stays local

Nothing syncs anywhere. Your client details live in your own browser storage, on your own machine. Clear it and the data is gone — that's the trade.

Limits stated upfront

Every listing says what it doesn't do before you buy. No recurring billing, no client portals, no invoice database. If you need those, buy a hosted service instead.

Straight answers

Do these work outside the UK?
Most do, with one exception worth saying plainly: Late Payment Desk uses UK statutory interest rules, which don't apply elsewhere. The other tools are jurisdiction-neutral. Every listing states its limits before you buy.
Who made these?
I did — one person, for my own client work. Oskar May is a pen name. Every product here is something I use or have used myself.
How does delivery work?
Payment and delivery go through Gumroad. You get a ZIP containing the file and a README with setup instructions.
What if it doesn't work?
Reply to your receipt and say what happened. Real answer, from a person. If something is broken I'll fix it or refund it.
Can I use these with clients?
Yes. Personal and commercial use. Modify them freely — the tools are plain HTML, the guides are plain text. Just don't resell them as-is.
